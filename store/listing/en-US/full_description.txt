Wren carries a lantern into Glimmerdeep, a cave where nothing is pushed and nothing is fought. The creatures move themselves, and they move by the light. You decide where the light is.

Fluffmoths fly toward the lantern. Shellwhelks creep away from it. Stumpcaps only walk while they are lit, and dozer newts only wander in the dark. An Emberwick kept in the light catches and follows Wren as a second little light. A Gloamling drifts after the lantern and eats the light around it, and a Mirrorbeetle turns the lantern's beam round corners. Plant the lantern, pick it up again, and bring every corner of the cavern into the glow.

What is inside
• 133 caverns in seven chapters, each chapter ending with a Gloam encounter, where the Hush creeps over every dark tile and only light holds it back
• The Daily Glimmer: one cavern a day, the same for everyone, 365 in all
• The Night Garden: 30 deep puzzles built round crystal walls, which let light through but nothing that walks
• The Workshop: paint your own caverns, let the solver find their par, and share them as a short code
• Wren's Field Journal: the story of the dimming Heartroot, a page for every creature you meet, and hidden critters to sketch
• Lantern colours to earn with star moss

Gentle by design
• Undo any move, as often as you like
• Hints that help a step at a time: first the key creature, then the first move, then the whole way
• No timers, no lives, no energy and no ads
• Every cavern is checked by a solver, so every one can be lit, and par is always within reach

Play it your way
• Larger text and a plain, readable font
• Motion that follows your phone, stays still, or stays on
• Arrow buttons for anyone who would rather not swipe, and a left-handed layout
• Colours chosen to stay clear with every type of colour vision
• TalkBack reads the whole board, and Board talk speaks each move aloud

What it costs
Chapter 1 is free to play. One payment, the Full Expedition, opens chapters 2 to 7 and the Night Garden. Glimmer jars buy instant hints if you want them, but a free hint comes every ten minutes anyway.

Private
Glimmerdeep collects nothing and sends nothing. Your progress stays on your phone, and purchases go through Google Play.
