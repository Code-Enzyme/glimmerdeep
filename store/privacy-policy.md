# Glimmerdeep privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Glimmerdeep collects nothing and sends nothing. Your progress stays on your phone, and purchases go through Google Play.

Glimmerdeep is a puzzle game for Android made by Code Enzyme. This page says what the game keeps, where it keeps it, and what leaves your phone. The short answer is: nothing leaves it unless you send it yourself.

## What the game keeps

The game keeps your progress on your phone: the caverns you have lit and your best moves, star moss, the critters you have sketched, the story pages you have read, your settings, the caverns you make in the Workshop, how many Glimmer you have, and the tester notes (how many times you opened, lit, undid, restarted and asked for a hint in each cavern, and roughly how long you spent there).

There are no accounts, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The game does not read your contacts, photos, location or anything else on your phone, and it does not send your progress or anything about you to us or to anyone else.

Your progress is deleted when you uninstall the game, clear its storage, or choose Reset progress in the game.

## Purchases

The Full Expedition and Glimmer jars are sold through Google Play. Google takes the payment and handles your payment details under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The game only learns which purchases you own; it never sees your card or account details.

## Things you choose to share

A Workshop share code holds the cavern itself, and nothing else. Tester notes stay on your phone until you copy them. Either one goes only where you paste it yourself; the game never sends them.

Board talk hands the words it speaks to your phone's own text-to-speech, the same way any app that talks does.

## Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address, with a new date at the top.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, write to the developer email address shown on Glimmerdeep's Google Play page.
